How to play Blokus

Four colours share one 20×20 board. On your turn you drop one polyomino.

  • Your first piece must cover your starting corner.
  • Every later piece must touch another of your own pieces at a corner (diagonally).
  • It may never share a full edge with your own colour — edges of other colours are fine.
  • Rotate and flip freely. If you can’t move, you pass.

The game ends when nobody can move. You score −1 per remaining square, so play your big pieces early. Clearing all 21 pieces earns +15 (+20 if your last piece was the single square). Highest score wins.
